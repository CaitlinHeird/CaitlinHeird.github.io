About Guiding Compass

Guiding Compass is a nonprofit animal shelter dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming dogs and cats in need. We believe every animal deserves safety, care, and a loving home.

How We Help Animals

1. Rescue

We take in animals from shelters, strays, and urgent situations.

2. Care

Every animal receives medical treatment, food, and rehabilitation.

3. Rehome

We match pets with families based on personality and needs.

Why Adopt From Us?

  • All pets are health-checked and vaccinated
  • Behavior assessments for better matching
  • Post-adoption support and guidance
  • No-kill shelter commitment

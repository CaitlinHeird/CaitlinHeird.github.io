How do I adopt?

  1. Browse available animals
  2. Submit an adoption application
  3. Meet your potential pet
  4. Complete approval process
  5. Take your new pet home

Our team will guide you through every step to make sure it’s a great match for both you and the animal.

What to do if you find a stray?

  • Do not chase or corner the animal
  • Check for a collar or ID tag if safe
  • Keep distance and observe behavior
  • Contact local animal control or our shelter
  • If safe, provide water and temporary shelter

If the animal appears injured or aggressive, contact emergency services immediately.

How can I help if I can’t donate?

There are many ways to support our animals without giving money.

  • Volunteer at the shelter
  • Foster an animal temporarily
  • Donate supplies (food, blankets, toys)
  • Share adoptable pets on social media
  • Attend or help with events
